1. **Kenneth H. Rosen, Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications (8th Edition).**
   - Comprehensive introduction to sequences, recurrence relations, and their applications.

2. **Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, Clifford Stein, Introduction to Algorithms (3rd Edition).**
   - Standard algorithms for finding increasing and decreasing subsequences.

3. **Wikipedia: Sequence**
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequence
   - Theory, types, and examples of sequences.

4. **Wikipedia: Longest Increasing Subsequence**
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longest_increasing_subsequence
   - Algorithms and applications for increasing subsequences.

5. **NPTEL: Discrete Mathematics**
   - https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111/106/111106086/
   - Video lectures and notes on sequences and recurrence relations.
